Scientific & Potability Disclaimer: Total Dissolved Solids (TDS) measures the total mass of dissolved inorganic salts and organic matter, and the DS18B20 measures physical thermal energy. TDS and temperature alone DO NOT establish whether water is chemically or biologically safe to drink. They do not test for micro-organisms, viral pathogens, toxic pesticides, lead, arsenic, or chlorine. Standardized laboratory testing is required for drinking water potability certification.
